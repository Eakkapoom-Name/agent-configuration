### SDD Workflow & Grilling Selection
When executing non-trivial tasks, SDD lifecycle, or spawning subagents:
- Use `/grill-me` (stateless interview) for non-code planning, idea sharpening, or general design.
- Use `/grill-with-docs` (stateful interview with `CONTEXT.md` & ADR paper trail) for coding, codebase feature planning, and technical architecture.
